  HOW TO WRITE ENTRIES  (read this before adding any — it is easy to get wrong)

  The game gives the SETUP. The players deliver the punchline. If a line is
  already funny on its own, it has done the players' job for them and there is
  nothing left to argue about.

  Shortlist items: 2-4 words. A bare thing. No commas, no "that", no "which".
  All seven items in a theme should be the same shape (all nouns, or all
  gerunds) so they can be compared at a glance — ranking seven items is 21
  separate comparisons, so every extra word costs.

      no    the first coffee, before anyone speaks to them
      no    the shower that fogs up the whole flat
      yes   coffee
      yes   long showers

  One modifier at most, never two. The moment you add a clause explaining WHY
  something is funny, you have written the joke instead of the players.

  Never write the detail that proves you know them. Write the flat thing
  they'll fight over. Warmth comes from players defending themselves, not from
  the writer being tender on their behalf.

  Banned: em-dash asides, "specifically", "the good one", "non-negotiable".
  That's the writer winking at the reader.

  Spice: the mix is deliberate. Most entries are safe, some are flirty, a few
  are genuinely rude. Keep that spread when adding more.

  Audience: any couple — three months in or thirty years. Don't assume they
  live together, own a house, or have a pet.
