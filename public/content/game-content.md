- Overrated | Underrated

- Fine at a wedding | Absolutely not at a wedding
- Red flag | Green flag
- Normal amount to spend | Absurd amount to spend
- Effort | Far too much effort
- Would admit to it | Taking it to the grave
- Terrible gift | Excellent gift
- Easy to forgive | Never getting over it
- Childish | Distinguished
- Fine to be late for | Never be late for
- Safe to text at 2am | Do not text at 2am

- Reasonable to cry at | Get a grip
